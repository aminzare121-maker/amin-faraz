# V5 opening-gap fade (2026-11-18)

New hypothesis: a large difference between previous M15 close and current M15 open may revert on the next bar as liquidity returns. Data: private Eplanet M15 CSV, 23643 bars. 60/40 chronological split, selected gap threshold 0.5 x median prior 48 ranges.

Out-of-sample 104 trading days: baseline 38 opportunities, 38 trades, gross +$423.20, modeled costs $316.00, net +$107.20, max closed-equity drawdown $184.70, 4 days >= $50. Risk filter (spread <=35 points and gap <=1.5x median range): 38 opportunities, 1 trade, gross +$187.40, costs $5.20, net +$182.20, 1 day >= $50. Capital $1000, variable lots up to 0.10.

**Not approved for demo**: guard sample size is one, and baseline misses daily target. Spread column interpreted as $0.01 per point, plus $0.20/oz roundtrip slippage. No commission, stop execution, intrabar excursion or true broker fills modeled; therefore results are preliminary and could be overstated. No private market data committed.
